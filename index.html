THE SEVENTEENTH OFFENSIVE

NEATHER EARTHREBUILT

ZX SPECTRUM RECONSTRUCTION
TITLE

Capture or destroy the three Insignian warbases. Keep Kerberus in human hands.

FLIGHT CONTROLS

↑↓←→ or Q A O P to move.
Space to climb. Release to descend.

COMMAND

Land on the H at your warbase to construct robots.
Land on a friendly robot to give orders or take control.

MENUS

↑↓ to choose. Space to select.
In construction, ←→ choose Exit / Start / Parts.
